# Hook Mistakes — Key Points (Kallaway)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2byPP_9F0-Q

---

## A winning hook does ONE job:
Help the viewer decide to opt in and continue watching. It delivers:
1. **Topic clarity** — crystal clear understanding of what the video is about
2. **On-target curiosity** — they believe the topic is for them and are curious about what comes next

If you deliver both, you win. All 4 mistakes below break one or both.

---

## Mistake 1: DELAY

**Problem:** Topic context comes too late. First sentences are fluff.

**Example of bad delay:** "Guys, this is one of the craziest things I've ever seen. And when you see it, you're never going to believe it." — Zero topic context. Could be about anything.

**Fix:** Move topic intro to the first 1-2 seconds. Use the fewest words possible.

**Good example:** "Here are three simple ways to improve your gut health" or "If you have gut issues, these three remedies will help immediately."

**Key insight:** Retention chart is exponential decay. Every second without topic clarity = massive viewer loss. Some vague hooks "work" on TikTok but only because of how the creator looks or the text hook — the spoken words add zero value.

---

## Mistake 2: CONFUSION (Comprehension Loss)

**Problem:** Words or phrasing not clear enough. Viewer only understands half of what you're saying.

**Bad:** "These guys built a $30 million empire and the online money they made is most difficult to earn if you don't develop a journaling practice like they did."

**Good:** "These guys built a $30 million empire and their secret for earning money online was their insane journaling practice."

**Fix:**
- Fewer words (but enough they can't misunderstand)
- Simpler words (6th grade reading level)
- Active voice ("The dog jumped" not "The jump was done by the dog")
- **Clarity test:** Read the hook in isolation. Is there more than one way to interpret it? If yes, rewrite.

---

## Mistake 3: IRRELEVANCE

**Problem:** Viewer knows what the video is about, but doesn't feel it applies to THEM.

**Fix 1:** Use "you/your" instead of "I/me"
- Bad: "I've struggled with skin problems my whole life"
- Good: "If you've struggled with skin problems your whole life..."
- Using "you" makes the video feel targeted directly at them

**Fix 2:** Frame around expected value — agitate a pain point they already have
- Bad: "These are three common trends in skincare" (nice to have)
- Good: "If you struggle with acne, try these three things" (need to have)
- Need-to-have > nice-to-have

---

## Mistake 4: DISINTEREST (No Curiosity)

**Problem:** Clear topic, feels relevant, but the hook just doesn't grab them.

**Fix:** Build a curiosity loop through CONTRAST.

**Contrast** = distance between viewer's current belief and your contrarian alternative.

Two types:
1. **Stated contrast** — explicitly share both A and B: "Most people solve acne with Accutane, but I have an herbal remedy that does it 3x faster"
2. **Implied contrast** — only state your alternative, baseline is assumed: "This herbal supplement is 8x more effective" (viewer already knows the baseline)

**Structure:** Topic clarity in first sentence → contrast/curiosity in next 1-2 sentences.
