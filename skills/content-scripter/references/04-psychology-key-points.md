# 18 Psychology Tricks — Key Points (Kallaway)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_6TIu7zHDw

---

## The 6 Checkpoints (viewer must pass through all 6)

Every video = 6 gates. Miss one = they bounce.

---

### Checkpoint 1: PAIN ACCEPTANCE (first few seconds)

Get the viewer to FEEL the pain you're solving. All core pain stems from: money, time, health, or access.

**4 hacks:**

1. **One standard deviation away** — Don't target the core pain directly (triggers BS detector). Go one level adjacent and let them make the leap. Example: Don't say "make more money" — say "grow on social media" (they'll connect it to money themselves).

2. **Twist the knife** — Remind them of their frustration bluntly and directly. FOMO and jealousy work great. "If you've been struggling with storytelling, I've been there. I know how hard it is."

3. **Audience of one** — Use "you" not "I". Make THEM the main character. Talk like you're speaking to one person, not a crowd. Tone of a friend, not a preacher. (Kallaway uses "you" 9 times in his hook, "I" zero times.)

4. **Pain-solution gap** — Show the distance between where they are and where they want to be. Can use hands, graphics, or verbal examples showing the gap.

---

### Checkpoint 2: TRUST (3-30 seconds after hook)

They accepted the pain — now do they trust YOU to solve it?

**Trust builders (most to least effective):**
1. Proof you solved it for someone like them (testimonials, case studies)
2. Proof you solved it for yourself
3. Massive social following / pre-existing cosign
4. Evidence you've been in their shoes
5. Common ground / rapport
6. Professional setup (studio, audio, confidence)

**Key:** If you have any of the top 3, you're probably good. If not, stack multiple of the bottom 3.
**Bigger ask = more trust needed. Deeper pain = less trust needed.**

**Quick hacks:** Flash screenshots/proof rapidly (1-2 is enough). Say "I've been exactly where you are." Professional setup inverts skepticism — they root for you.

---

### Checkpoint 3: PLAN OF ATTACK (right after trust)

They trust you — now do you have a plan?

**2 things needed:**

1. **Point of difference** — Create contrast between what they've commonly seen and your contrarian take. Brain constantly seeks easier/better/faster. If you suggest something different = they HOLD to see what it is.

2. **Structured list** — Present your plan as a numbered list. "My 5 tips for X", "18 psychology hacks". Brain is good at chunking steps. Without structure = confused = churn. Numbers help them remember → feel smart → associate feeling smart with you → trust you more.

---

### Checkpoint 4: LIKABILITY (throughout)

Do they want YOU specifically to solve it?

**3 hacks:**

1. **Energy up 50%** — People mirror energy. High energy is infectious. Record one line at a time to maintain energy throughout (prevents trailing off).

2. **Simplification** — Make them feel smart, not dumb. Repeat points twice: once technical, once simple with metaphor. When they understand you = they like you.

3. **Be yourself** — People detect characters. Authenticity comes through the camera. When you talk about things you genuinely care about, they sense it.

---

### Checkpoint 5: ATTENTION (body of video)

Can you hold them long enough to deliver the value?

**3 hacks:**

1. **Create a hunt** — Lead with most valuable piece first (dopamine release, validates staying). Tease gold ahead: "Wait for the end, the last one's going to be great." Opens long-term loops while closing mini ones.

2. **Rehooking** — Keep reigniting the pain-solution gap. Use "and-flip": "Now that was good, but if you don't figure out this next part, it's never going to work." Zigzag dance of contrast.

3. **Trance rhythm** — Vary sentence lengths. Same-length sentences = monotony = boredom = churn. Mix short staccato with longer legato. Look at your script — if the right edge isn't jagged, your sentences are too uniform.

---

### Checkpoint 6: ACTION (CTA)

Can you get them to DO something?

**3 hacks:**

1. **Close the loop** — Tell them you've successfully closed the pain-solution gap. They have everything they need. BUT if they want speed, they can take your action/buy your product. Reminds them the pain is still unsolved (they haven't absorbed 100%) → triggers action.

2. **Unexpected surprise** — Reality > expectations = action. If your video blew away what they expected, they're way more likely to act. Deliver at 12/10 quality.

3. **Recap** — Tell them what you're going to do, do it, remind them what you did. Final nail that closes the loop and reminds them to take action.
