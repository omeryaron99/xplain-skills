## 3 Script Writing Mistakes

### Mistake 1: BAD STORY FLOW

**Problem:** Too many unnecessary details break the through line. Extra context, disconnected tangents, broken plots, distractions.

**Why it happens:** People get excited about topics they know and overshare to prove expertise. They think more = better. Wrong.

**Storytelling = hypnosis.** You want the viewer fully immersed in one single plot. Every unnecessary detail makes that hypnosis more fragile. More breaks = more churn.

**Fix:** After writing your script, read line by line and ask: "Is this line a necessary step in the logic flow, or is it a distraction?" Keep necessary lines, delete distractions.

**The test principle:** Like a word bank on a test — not every word is meant to be used. Only use facts/details that are necessary for comprehension.

---

### Mistake 2: COMPREHENSION PROBLEM

**Problem:** Viewer doesn't understand the words/phrasing. They feel dumb. They bounce.

**Goal is NOT** to demonstrate how much more you know than them.
**Goal IS** to demonstrate how big of a gap you can help them close. Make THEM feel smart.

**5 tactical fixes:**

1. **Simpler words** — Target 6th grade vocabulary. Big words only valuable to people who know them.
2. **Fewer words** — Shorter sentences. Less brain load. Quick, punchy, staccato.
3. **Active voice** — "The dog jumped" not "The jumping was done by the dog"
4. **Restate twice** — Say it technical, then repeat in simplest possible terms. "Rudimentary... or simple."
5. **Name your frameworks** — People relate to a single word name more than a long definition. "Atomic messaging", "story flow" — just made up, but easier to remember.

---

### Mistake 3: SPEED TO VALUE

**Problem:** Value or mention of value comes too late before viewer bounces.

**Key insight:** Social media content ≠ traditional stories. Books/movies assume captive audience. Social media = you earned a lucky slot machine pull and must prove value before they lose interest.

**The script flow for maximum speed to value:**

```
HOOK (0-3 sec) — Context + agitate pain or tease benefit
BODY 1 — Clear story flow, only critical details
REHOOK (~20-25 sec for short form, ~2-3 min for YouTube)
  → Re-agitate the pain, signal better value still coming
  → "Now that was good, but what's coming next is even better"
BODY 2 — More value, clear flow
[Repeat REHOOK → BODY as needed]
OUTRO — Summarize value stated OR extend value ramp (lead magnet, link)
```

**Rehook phrases:** "But here's where it gets crazy...", "That's cool, but watch this part", "And that's not even the best part"

**For short form:** Probably 1-2 rehooks max over 60-90 seconds
**For YouTube:** 4-5 rehooks
