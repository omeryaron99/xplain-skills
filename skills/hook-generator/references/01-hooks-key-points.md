## The 3-Step Hook Formula (Curiosity Loop)

### Step 1: Context Lean (first 1-2 sentences)
- **Topic clarity** — viewer must know what the video is about immediately so they can self-select in or out
- **Get them leaning in** — use one of these tactics:
  - Establish common ground
  - Reference a benefit or pain point they have
  - Use a metaphor to simplify a complex idea
  - Tell them something that blows their mind

### Step 2: Scroll Stop Interjection (1 sentence)
- Acts like a stun gun — stops forward momentum
- Use a contrasting word: "but", "however", "yet", "although", "therefore", "on the other hand"
- This is the setup for the haymaker in step 3

### Step 3: Contrarian Snapback (1-2 sentences)
- Goes in the OPPOSITE direction of the initial lean
- Still on topic but snaps the viewer onto a different path
- Bigger shock = bigger snap = better effect
- After this, viewer is fully hooked and can't stop watching

**Example (Sphere video, 8M views):**
1. Context lean: "The tech in the Vegas sphere is insane. Biggest screen ever built, 20 times bigger than an IMAX."
2. Scroll stop: "But get this — the screen is actually the least impressive part."
3. Snapback: "Because the most impressive part is the audio. This is going to blow your mind."

**Works in every category** — entertainment, B2B, real estate, education. Same psychology, different topic.

## Visual Hooks (100x more powerful than spoken hooks)

- People read faster than they hear — text on screen gives instant context
- **Text overlay:** 3-5 words, big bold font, helps build context lean
- **Visual motion:** Just enough to hold attention. Too much = overwhelm. Too little = boring.
- The combo of text + motion + spoken word = way more powerful than spoken word alone

## Common Ground Tactics

### Lead with benefit/pain, not the topic
- Weak: "You should take magnesium because it's one of 21 core minerals"
- Strong: "If you want better sleep, you need magnesium"
- Lead with THEIR pain/benefit, then introduce the solution

### Cult Hopping
- Wrap unknown ideas in known references (brands, celebrities, cultural moments)
- Creates subconscious comfort — people feel smart when they recognize something
- Use as metaphor/comparison, don't build whole video around it

---

## Speed to Value

- Short form: ~4 second timer before they bounce
- YouTube: ~1-2 minute timer
- Frontload value as much as possible — give a hit right at the hook or immediately after
- Anything after they click off might as well be a black screen
- Value loop: context → immediate value → context → immediate value

---

## Staccato Hooks

- Use short, punchy sentences in the hook
- Shorter sentences = max clarity per word
- Increases density of value per word at the beginning when time is most critical
- Start staccato, then expand to medium/longer sentences as video continues
